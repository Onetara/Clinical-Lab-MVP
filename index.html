Bench Shifts (Production efficiency per lab bench)

Shifts in EST: Night 0000-0800, Morning 0800-1600, Afternoon 1600-0000. A sample belongs to the shift in which its bench received it.

  • Raw score: finished in shift ÷ received in shift (how benches are graded today).
  • Late arrivals: reached the bench with less shift time left than the bench normally needs (more tests on a sample means more time needed). They are left out of the fair score.
  • Fair score: the same calculation without those late arrivals. The gap shows how much of the score came from delivery and handoff delays, not the tech.
